From floor plan to physical port

Keep the installation understandable after the job is finished — without rebuilding it from paper notes, phone photos or memory.

Any plan becomes the map

Upload a floor plan or photo, or simply photograph a paper plan and start placing infrastructure directly on it.

Outlet-to-port connection

Link a field point to the exact cabinet, patch panel and physical port — and move it later when the installation changes.

Cabinets & patch panels

See panels, port capacity and which physical ports are already connected or still available.

Installation status

Track progress from cable pulled through installation and testing, so the next technician sees the current state immediately.

Photos & notes

Add field photos and comments to points so problems, routing details and site context stay attached to the right location.

Who changed what

See who created a point or cabinet and who last modified it, with creation and last-update timestamps.

Workflow

Four simple steps

The current prototype already covers the core workflow from site plan to a documented physical connection.

01 — MAP

Load the site plan

Use an existing drawing, screenshot or a phone photo as the working map.

02 — PLACE

Add field objects

Place outlets, Wi‑Fi, cameras and cabinets directly where they exist on site.

03 — CONNECT

Assign the exact port

Connect each point to its cabinet, patch panel and physical port number.

04 — KEEP

Preserve the record

Status, photos, notes and change metadata stay with the project for the next visit.
