How it works

A simple workflow from plan to port

The current prototype already covers the core field process from creating the map to preserving the final port-level record.

01 — ADD

Add a plan

Upload a floor plan, screenshot or site photo and use it as the working map.

02 — MAP

Map points

Place outlets, cabinets, cameras and Wi‑Fi exactly where they exist on site.

03 — DEFINE

Define ports

Add patch panels and assign every field point to its exact cabinet and physical port.

04 — USE

Use it in the field

Find, verify, move and update everything from your phone during installation or maintenance.
