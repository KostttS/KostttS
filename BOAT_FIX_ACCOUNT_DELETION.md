# Boat Fix — Account & Data Deletion

**Last updated: September 20, 2026**

This page provides a way for users of **Boat Fix** to request deletion of their app account and associated personal data.

## Request account deletion

Send an email to:

**oxta.ha@gmail.com**

Use the subject:

**Boat Fix Account Deletion**

Please include:
- The email address associated with your Boat Fix account.
- A clear statement that you want your Boat Fix account deleted.

[Start an account deletion request by email](mailto:oxta.ha@gmail.com?subject=Boat%20Fix%20Account%20Deletion)

We may ask for limited additional information to verify that the request is being made by the account owner.

## What will be deleted

After verification, the deletion process covers personal data associated with the Boat Fix account, including:
- Authentication account information.
- Account-linked profile information.
- Claim or business-access request information associated with the account.
- Other personal data linked specifically to the account, unless retention is legally or operationally required.

## What may remain

Public business-directory information that exists independently of the account, including information obtained from public sources, may remain in Boat Fix after account deletion.

If you also want public business-listing information corrected or removed, state this separately in your request and identify the business listing.

Certain records may be retained where reasonably necessary for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution. Any retained data will be limited to what is necessary for that purpose.

## Processing time

Account-deletion requests are normally completed within **30 days** after any necessary identity verification.
