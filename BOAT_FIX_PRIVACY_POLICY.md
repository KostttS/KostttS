# Boat Fix Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 20, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Boat Fix** ("Boat Fix", "the App") collects, uses, stores, and shares information.

**Developer / data controller:** Konstantin Sergeev  
**Privacy contact:** oxta.ha@gmail.com

## 1. What Boat Fix does

Boat Fix helps users find marinas and boat-service providers and allows eligible business representatives to create an account, claim a business listing, and manage business information.

## 2. Information we process

Depending on how you use the App, Boat Fix may process the following information:

### Account information
- Email address.
- Authentication account identifier and session information.
- Information needed to verify access to a business account.

Passwords are handled by the authentication service and are not stored by Boat Fix in readable form.

### Business listing and claim information
If you represent a business, the App may process information such as:
- Business name.
- Business email address.
- Phone number.
- Website.
- Address and city.
- Service categories.
- Business description.
- Business photos.
- Claim or access-request information.

Some business-directory information may come from publicly available sources and may be visible to users without an account.

### Location information
With your permission, Boat Fix may access your device location to show nearby marinas and service providers and to calculate or sort results by distance.

Location coordinates may be sent to the App's backend solely to perform nearby searches and related location-based functionality. Boat Fix does not use location data for advertising.

You can deny or revoke location permission through your device settings. Some nearby-search features may then be unavailable.

### Technical and security information
The App and its infrastructure providers may process limited technical information needed to operate, secure, diagnose, and maintain the service, such as request logs, error information, network information, and authentication/security events.

## 3. How information is used

Boat Fix uses information to:
- Provide marina and boat-service search.
- Show nearby results.
- Authenticate business users.
- Verify and process business-claim requests.
- Allow authorized business representatives to manage listings.
- Provide customer support.
- Prevent abuse and protect the security of the service.
- Diagnose technical problems and improve reliability.

Boat Fix does not sell personal information and does not use personal information for third-party advertising.

## 4. Service providers and sharing

Boat Fix uses third-party infrastructure providers to operate the App. In particular, **Supabase** may process data for database, authentication, API, and storage functions.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the App, or the developer from fraud, abuse, or security threats.

Boat Fix does not share personal data with data brokers.

## 5. Data security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information, including authenticated access, database access controls, secure network connections, and permission-based access to protected business-management functions.

No system can guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Data retention

Account and claim-related personal data is retained while needed to provide the service and for a reasonable period required for security, dispute resolution, or legal obligations.

When an account-deletion request is completed, personal data associated with that account is deleted or anonymized unless retention is required for a legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or compliance reason.

Public business-directory information that exists independently of a user account, including information obtained from public sources, may remain in the directory after an account is deleted. A business may separately request correction or removal of inaccurate listing information.

## 7. Account and data deletion

Users may request deletion of their Boat Fix account and associated personal data.

To request deletion, visit:

[Boat Fix Account & Data Deletion](https://github.com/KostttS/KostttS/blob/main/BOAT_FIX_ACCOUNT_DELETION.md)

You may also email **oxta.ha@gmail.com** with the subject **"Boat Fix Account Deletion"** and include the email address associated with the Boat Fix account.

Deletion requests are normally processed within 30 days after any necessary identity verification.

## 8. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of your personal data, and to object to certain processing.

For location data, you can grant or revoke device permission at any time through your operating system settings.

For privacy requests, contact **oxta.ha@gmail.com**.

## 9. Legal bases for users in the EEA/UK

Where applicable, Boat Fix processes personal data on one or more of the following bases:
- To provide services requested by the user or perform a contract.
- Legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving the service.
- Consent, where required, including device permission for location access.
- Compliance with legal obligations.

## 10. Children's privacy

Boat Fix is not directed to children and is intended for users who can lawfully use the service and, where applicable, represent a business.
