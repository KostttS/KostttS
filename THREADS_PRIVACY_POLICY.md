# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 18, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the Threads API integration operated by Konstantin ("the App") handles information.

## What the App does

The App is used to manage and analyze a Threads account, monitor replies and engagement, and search public Threads content for relevant conversations and opportunities.

## Information processed

Depending on the features used, the App may process:

- Public Threads posts and replies, including post text, username, post ID, permalink, and timestamp.
- Information associated with the connected Threads account that is made available through the Threads API, such as profile information, published posts, replies, and engagement/insight data.
- Search results returned by the Threads API for keyword searches.

The App does not intentionally collect passwords, payment-card information, or other sensitive authentication credentials. Threads access tokens are stored separately as protected secrets and are not published in the source repository.

## How information is used

Information is used only to:

- publish and manage content for the connected Threads account;
- monitor replies and engagement;
- analyze account performance;
- search public Threads content for relevant discussions and potential business opportunities;
- prepare appropriate replies and follow-up actions.

## Storage and retention

Operational data may be stored in application data files and service logs for as long as reasonably necessary to operate, troubleshoot, and improve the App.

## Sharing

The App does not sell personal information.

Information is not shared with third parties for advertising or data-broker purposes. Data may be processed by infrastructure providers used to operate the App, such as GitHub and Meta, subject to their own terms and privacy policies.
