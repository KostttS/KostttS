Contact Support

If you need help with Boat Fix, have a problem with a business listing, or want to report incorrect information, contact us by email.

Email Boat Fix Support
Email: kors-film@ya.ru

What we can help with

  • Problems signing in or creating a business account
  • Claiming or managing a business listing
  • Incorrect marina or service provider information
  • Location or search issues
  • Photo or business information updates
  • General app questions and technical problems

When contacting us

Please include a short description of the issue and, if relevant, the marina or business name. Screenshots can also help us investigate the problem faster.
